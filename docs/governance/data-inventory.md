# Data inventory and record of processing: work-order tracker

This inventory lists every dataset and flow of personal data in the system, with its storage location, retention period and deletion method, and the lawful basis for each use of personal data. For personal data, it also serves as the record of processing activities required by Article 30 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Nine datasets contain personal data. Work-order history (D-6), logs (D-8), access tokens (D-9), idempotency records (D-11), failed login records (D-12) and the audit log (D-13) are included because they identify users even though no one types names into them. The synthetic dataset (D-10) contains invented people only and is generated the same way on every run. Backups of the database are kept for 30 days on a rolling basis in object storage outside the cluster, in the same region as the cluster, and expire automatically. Past job records in the existing spreadsheets and paper job cards are not imported: they are incomplete and inconsistent, and importing them would extend how long personal data about technicians is kept.

## Processing of personal data

| ID | Purpose | Lawful basis | Categories of people | Datasets | Recipients | International transfers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Give staff access according to their role | Contract (employment) | All staff | D-1, D-9 | Administrators | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |
| P-2 | Assign and track jobs | Contract (employment) | Technicians, supervisors, coordinators | D-5 | Coordinators, supervisors and the operations manager; each technician sees their own jobs | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |
| P-3 | Contact customers about jobs | Legitimate interests | Customer facility managers | D-3 | Coordinators, supervisors; the assigned technician, for their own jobs | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |
| P-4 | Keep an accountable record of changes and settle disputes | Legitimate interests | All staff who change jobs | D-6 | Operations manager, managing director | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |
| P-5 | Report SLA performance by customer, trade and team | Legitimate interests | Technicians and supervisors, in aggregate | D-5, D-6 | Operations manager, managing director | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |
| P-6 | Keep the system secure and investigate faults | Legitimate interests | All users | D-8 | Operations manager, system support | None in development or the pilot, which hold synthetic data only |

Each row is one purpose for which personal data is used. Uses based on legitimate interests are each supported by a written legitimate interests assessment. Production hosting either keeps data in Nigeria or relies on a lawful transfer mechanism under section 41 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, as recorded in the data protection impact assessment. SLA reporting (P-5) is grouped by customer, trade and team; it does not rank individual technicians.

## Applicability

- **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) applies:** yes. All people whose data is held are in Lagos, Nigeria.
- **GDPR applies:** not by law, because no services are offered to, and no behaviour is monitored of, people in the European Union. The system is designed to meet GDPR requirements.

## Lineage

| Output | Built from | Transformations |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly SLA compliance report | D-4, D-5, D-6 | Compare each job's arrival and completion times with its response and resolution due times; exclude cancelled jobs and customer-caused holds; flag corrected times; group by customer, trade and team |
| Weekly record completeness report | D-5 | Count breakdowns missing a reported, arrival or completion time |
| Monthly billable-extras list | D-4, D-5 | Select jobs flagged billable or beyond the contract's included call-outs |

The SLA and billable-extras outputs answer the two largest costs in the engagement brief: service credits and unbilled extras. The completeness report shows whether the records behind them can be trusted. SLA due times are set by the system from the contract terms and working calendar (D-4), not typed by hand.
